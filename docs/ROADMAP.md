# DW-TADS Architectural Roadmap: Tier C Aspirational Capabilities

This document outlines the **Tier C** future capabilities and enterprise/defense-grade integrations planned for subsequent evolutionary phases of the Dark Web Threat Actor De-Anonymization System (DW-TADS).

These capabilities represent state-of-the-art national-security and intelligence-agency-grade extensions that require dedicated distributed hardware clusters, international legal accords, physical human intelligence operations, and live blockchain consensus nodes.

---

## 1. Distributed GPU Graph Neural Network (GNN) Training Cluster (`gnn-deanon`)

### Target Capability
- **Scalable Graph Embeddings**: Deploy a multi-node GPU cluster (PyTorch Geometric / DGL on NVIDIA H100s) training temporal inductive graph neural networks (e.g., T-GCN, RGCN) on graphs containing over 1 billion heterogeneous nodes (wallets, onion addresses, forum handles, clearnet hostnames).
- **Sub-graph Link Prediction**: Perform real-time inductive link prediction between seemingly disconnected darknet actor personas across temporal transaction and communication graphs.
- **Explainable De-Anonymization Paths**: Generate multi-hop sub-graph attributions with integrated GNNExplainer attribution paths presented directly in analyst investigations.

---

## 2. Live Physical HUMINT Source Dispatch & Verification Network (`humint-manager`)

### Target Capability
- **Covert Agent Communications**: End-to-end forward-secret, quantum-resistant cryptographically isolated messaging channels (Double Ratchet + Kyber-1024) for case officers and confidential informants.
- **Physical Dead-Drop & Asset Tracking**: Cryptographically verified physical dead-drop check-ins with localized tamper-evident sensor validation and geo-fenced duress codes.
- **Operational Security (OPSEC) Profiler**: Real-time evaluation of informant OPSEC health, alerting supervisors to anomalous behavior, burner phone re-use, or digital footprint leakage.

---

## 3. Automated International Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT) EDI Gateway (`mlat-coordinator`)

### Target Capability
- **Diplomatic Inter-Agency EDI**: Secure, automated Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) gateway interconnecting Interpol, Europol, DOJ OIA, and bilateral MLAT treaty signatory nations.
- **Automated Judicial Letters Rogatory**: Dynamic template generation and cryptographic notarization of court-admissible letters rogatory, preserving ISO/IEC 27037 chain-of-custody across national borders.
- **Jurisdictional Routing & Data Sovereignty**: Automatic filtering and redaction of non-discoverable PII based on bilateral bilateral sovereign intelligence-sharing pacts.

---

## 4. Zero-Knowledge Proof Query & Verifiable Computation Layer (`zkp-query-layer`)

### Target Capability
- **ZK-SNARK / STARK Identity Queries**: Enable analysts to query intelligence repositories (e.g., "Does this suspect appear in classified Case #402?") and receive mathematical zero-knowledge proof of existence without exposing confidential case details or compromising other investigations.
- **Cross-Agency Verifiable Computations**: Allow partner intelligence agencies to verify model inference integrity and evidence hash chains without accessing proprietary underlying investigative models or raw surveillance logs.

---

## 5. Autonomous Multi-Agent Counter-Infiltration & Honeytokens (`autonomous-agent`)

### Target Capability
- **Persona Emulation**: Deploy LLM-driven autonomous agents capable of sustained interaction in restricted dark web marketplaces and invite-only criminal forums to establish authentic reputation scores.
- **Canary & Honeytoken Injection**: Dynamically craft and inject unique, trackable cryptographic honeytokens (e.g., Canary tokens, tailored leaked database schemas) into underground markets to identify buyer identities and downstream exfiltration vectors.
- **Counter-Deception Analysis**: Real-time evaluation of adversary counter-intelligence measures, detecting bot traps, captchas, and forum honeytokens aimed at investigative crawlers.

---

## 6. Live Sybil & Eclipse Network Collectors for Alternate Darknets (`i2p-collector`, `zeronet-collector`)

### Target Capability
- **I2P Floodfill DHT Sybil Infrastructure**: Deploy distributed cluster of high-bandwidth I2P floodfill routers to intercept and catalog private leasesets, unmasking hidden services through network topology mapping.
- **ZeroNet Tracker & BitTorrent DHT Infiltration**: Operate distributed nodes across ZeroNet tracker swarms to record peer IP addresses and swarm propagation metrics in real time.

---

## 7. Post-Quantum Cryptographic Merkle Anchoring (`evidence-anchor`)

### Target Capability
- **Post-Quantum Digital Signatures**: Transition evidence chain-of-custody seals from classical ECDSA/RSA to NIST-standardized Post-Quantum algorithms (ML-DSA / Dilithium and Falcon).
- **Public & Private Multi-Ledger Anchoring**: Anchor forensic Merkle tree roots concurrently into Bitcoin, Ethereum, and permissioned hyperledgers to guarantee mathematical immutability and indefinite evidentiary admissibility in court.
